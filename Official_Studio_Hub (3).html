African Dam Rules Manual

1. Flying Kings ("Dam"): Kings glide any unoccupied diagonal distance and capture in all 4 diagonal directions (including 90° turns, without reversing 180° over a piece just jumped).

2. Backward Pawn Captures: Regular pieces move diagonally forward only, but capture forward and backward.

3. Forced Pawns & King Huffing: Regular pieces must capture if able (you may pick any path). Kings are optional, but refusing a capture or stopping mid-chain removes ("Huffs") that King.

4. Win Condition: Win when the opponent has no legal moves or is reduced to 1 piece.

African Ludo Pro — House Rules

1. Yard Release & Bonus Rolls: Roll 6 (or 1/6) to deploy. Rolling 6, capturing, or reaching Goal grants a bonus roll. Triple-6 passes turn.

2. African Blockades & Locked Home: 2+ friendly seeds block opponents. Score 1 chop to unlock Home Corridor (🔓). While locked (🔒), loop around.

3. Reverse Chop & Home Line Raid: Chop enemies behind you by exact dice count, or raid an opponent's Home Corridor and return to their entrance.

4. Annual Journey vs Center Crown: Once unlocked, enter Home or stay on the outer track. Exact count enters Goal (step 56).
